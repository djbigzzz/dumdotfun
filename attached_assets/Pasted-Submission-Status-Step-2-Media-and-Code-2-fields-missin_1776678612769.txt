What are you building, and who is it for?
dum.fun is a Solana token launchpad where every newly minted token automatically gets a suite of prediction markets attached to it. Anyone can launch a meme token on a bonding curve in seconds - and from the moment it goes live, traders can bet YES/NO on questions like "Will the dev rug?", "Will it graduate to Raydium?", or "Will it survive 7 days?". Markets resolve automatically from on-chain signals (dev wallet behavior, holder counts, bonding-curve liquidity, graduation status) - no oracles, no human intervention.

Who it's for:

Degens who want a sharper game than blind aping into pump.fun clones - now they can short the rug.
Token launchers who want immediate price discovery and social signal on whether the market believes them.
Prediction-market traders looking for high-velocity, on-chain-resolvable markets that don't depend on slow oracle feeds.
Solana builders who can plug into our open APIs to spin up new market types, bots, dashboards, and integrations.
Why did you decide to build this, and why build it now?
I built dum.fun because I lost money on another launchpad, like 99% of the people who try. Bots are always in first, devs always rug, and even when you can see the rug coming, there's no way to actually make money from being right. You can either ape and hope or sit out and watch.

dum.fun fixes that. Every token has a prediction market attached. If you think the dev is going to rug, you bet on it and get paid when they do. You stop being a passive victim of the launchpad game and start playing it.

Why now: Solana finally makes on-chain auto-resolution cheap, the meme launchpad market just proved demand at scale, and FHE primitives let us build sealed bets so whales can't snipe each other.
What technologies are you using or integrating with to build your product?
Blockchain: Solana (Devnet), Token-2022, Anchor, Helius RPC, Raydium CPMM, Phantom wallet adapter.  Frontend: React, TypeScript, Vite, Wouter, TanStack Query, Tailwind, Framer Motion, Lightweight Charts.  Backend: Node.js, Express, PostgreSQL with Drizzle ORM, WebSocket server for live odds, on-chain auto-resolver service.  Integrations: SendGrid, IPFS for token metadata.  Frontier track (in progress): Encrypt FHE for sealed bets, Ika dWallets for cross-chain custody and threshold-signed resolut

Who else is building in this space, and what do you think they're getting wrong?
Three buckets of competitors, all missing the same thing.

Meme launchpads (pump.fun, moonshot, believe, daos.fun): great at launching, zero protection or instrument for the user. They've moved billions but their users have lost more. They treat rugs as an externality. We treat rugs as a market.

Prediction markets (Polymarket, Kalshi, Drift BET): excellent UX, but they focus on macro and politics. They don't speak crypto-native event types like rug detection, dev wallet behavior, or graduation outcomes. Their resolution is human-juried or oracle-fed and slow. Ours is fully on-chain and automatic.

Token analytics tools (Bubblemaps, Photon, GMGN): show you the rug risk but give you nothing actionable. They tell you the dev wallet looks suspicious, then leave you to either ape and pray or sit out.

Nobody has fused launchpad mechanics with native prediction markets and automatic on-chain resolution.  That gap is our wedge.
How do you make money, or how do you plan to?
Four streams, all on-chain and transparent:

Token creation fee: 0.05 SOL per launch
Market creation fee: 0.05 SOL per custom market
Trading fee: 1% on bonding curve buys and sells
Betting fee: 2% on prediction market bets
Pump.fun proved 1 to 2M USD per day in revenue at a 1% trade fee alone. Adding a 2% betting fee on top of every token's prediction markets is meaningful upside per active token, since bets compound across the token's full lifecycle, not just its trading window.
