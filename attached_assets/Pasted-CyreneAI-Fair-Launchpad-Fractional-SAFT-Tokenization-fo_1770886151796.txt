CyreneAI - Fair Launchpad & Fractional SAFT Tokenization for Early-Stage Builders
Overview
CyreneAI is a fair-launch fundraising platform that helps early-stage founders raise capital transparently by fractionalizing their SAFT/SAFE allocations and selling them through an open bonding-curve mechanism.

In the last 12 weeks, CyreneAI has helped early teams raise over $1.5 Million, making it one of the Top 4 launchpads on Solana (supported by Solana Foundation).
Our mission is to democratize access to early capital by allowing builders to tokenize their SAFT/SAFE allocation, raise instantly, and build in public - without relying on venture gatekeepers.

The New Model: Fractional SAFT/SAFE Tokenization
CyreneAI enables projects to tokenize a portion of their SAFT/SAFE allocation and sell it in fractional cliffs as capital is raised.
Example:
A project wants to raise $100K at a $10M valuation by selling 1% of their allocation.

On CyreneAI, this 1% is broken into 100 fractional cliffs - each representing 0.01% of the token allocation.
For every $1,000 raised, one cliff unlocks automatically and transfers ownership transparently to supporters.

By the end of the raise, the full 1% allocation is distributed.
This unlock model ensures:
Full transparency


No bulk dumping


Continuous proof-of-progress


Community-aligned vesting



Bonding Curve Sale + Transparent Liquidity
The fractional SAFT/SAFE tokens are sold through a bonding curve model, giving fair price discovery and guaranteed liquidity:
Initial liquidity: 101–201 SOL locked in the pool (varies per project)


Immediate trading: Fully open market from day one


Fees = founder earnings
1% → Project (revenue stream)
This ensures founders don’t receive all capital at once. Instead, they earn continuously as the market grows - incentivizing long-term building.
No upfront fees. No hidden charges. 100% transparent.
