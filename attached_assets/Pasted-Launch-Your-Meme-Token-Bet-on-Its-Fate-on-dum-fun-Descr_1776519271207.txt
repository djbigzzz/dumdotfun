The Challenge

Launch a meme token on dum.fun. Then let the community decide — will it survive, graduate, or rug?

dum.fun is a token launchpad where every token comes with built-in prediction markets. Create a token, trade it on the bonding curve, and bet on whether other tokens will make it or die.

🌐 App: dum.fun (https://dum-fun.replit.app/) (Solana Devnet)
💬 Telegram: t.me/dumfun
🐦 X: @dumfun

How It Works

1. Launch a token — pick a name, design a logo, write a description. Your token goes live on a bonding curve instantly.

2. Bet on tokens — every token has prediction markets:

• 🫧 Will it survive?
• 🏴‍☠️ Will it rug?
• 🎓 Will it graduate?
• 📊 Will it stay active?
• 💧 Will it keep liquidity?

Markets resolve automatically from on-chain data. No trust required.

3. Trade — buy tokens you believe in, sell ones you don't. The bonding curve sets the price.
