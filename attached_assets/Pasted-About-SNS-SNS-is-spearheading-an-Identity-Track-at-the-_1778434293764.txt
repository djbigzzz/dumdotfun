About SNS
SNS is spearheading an Identity Track at the Solana Network State Spring ‘26 event. We help builders create cutting-edge identity protocols on Solana's high-speed network. This initiative is part of the larger Frontier hackathon, a five-week program hosted in collaboration with Superteam MY, Network School.

Mission
Build an identity-focused project on Solana, leveraging social and agent identity concepts.

Hackathon Track Scope
Build a decentralized application (dApp) that explores identity, social identity, or agent identity on Solana.
This track focuses on how identity can serve as a core primitive for trust, interaction in onchain ecosystem. 
We will structure this track into two focuses themes:

Agent Identity: Build the onchain identity layer and source of trust using .sol for autonomous agent on Solana

Social Identity: Reimagine .sol as the universal identity and login layer for Solana applications. Focus on improving user onboarding, portability of identity, and composability across apps.

Projects could include:

On-chain reputation systems.

Social graph protocols that leverage Solana wallets for identity.

AI agents with distinct on-chain identities.

Tools that visualize or manage aspects of decentralized identity.

Solutions for sybil resistance in DAOs or airdrop campaigns using identity.

Submission Requirements
Register for the hackathon on the Colosseum platform and select "Global".

Submit your project to the Frontier hackathon on the Colosseum platform and select "Global"

Submit your project on both the Colosseum portal and Superteam Earn.

The project must meet the official rules of the global hackathon.

The overall submission quality (pitch deck, videos, and documentation) should clearly communicate the problem, product, architecture, and its significance.

A clear explanation of how your project addresses identity, social identity, or agent identity on Solana.

Submission must include a link to a public GitHub repository with your project's code. If the team’s Github repo is closed source, please grant access to contact@sns.id for our evaluation.

Must be in English.

Judging Criteria
Our team and local judging will be aligned with Colosseum’s founder-first philosophy. We will evaluate projects based on:

Innovation: How new and creative is the approach to identity on Solana?

Technical Merit: Quality of the code, smart contract design, and overall technical implementation.

Practicality/Use Case: How useful is the project, and what real-world problem does it solve?

Completeness: How well-developed is the project within the hackathon timeframe?

User Experience: How intuitive and easy to use is the dApp?

Founder Potential: Does the team look like it can keep shipping after the hackathon? Is there evidence of speed, clarity, and conviction?

Demo and Submission Quality: How clearly does the team communicate the problem, product, architecture, and why it matters?
